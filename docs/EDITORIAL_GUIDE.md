# Editorial guide

This is original fiction written by journalists inside the Common Reach. The tone is serious, attentive and occasionally amused. The reporting is not an account of real people or events and must be labeled as fictional on the publication, article and social preview.

## Voice and evidence

Use concrete decisions, objects and consequences. People inhabit this technology; they do not need every appliance explained. A kitchen table can carry as much narrative weight as a council chamber. Vary length and cadence. Forty curated articles include full features, short briefings, profiles, investigations, analysis and opinion. Avoid filler generation to meet a quota.

A canonical event is the source of established facts. An allegation is attributed and expressly unresolved. Opinion and analysis do not grant permission to invent factual claims. Not every disagreement is evidence of corruption. Private relationships require an on-record statement before coverage can treat them as established.

Free prose receives manual claim review. Exact structured claim validation proves that a listed claim matches a source event; it does not prove every sentence in an arbitrary draft is true. Editors must inspect unsupported detail, timeline implications, quotations, hidden plans and reputational allegations. The opening edition is curated authorial fiction; future proposed facts require event approval.

## Section conventions

| Desk | Convention |
| --- | --- |
| Interplanetary Affairs | Name the jurisdiction, route and communications delay; distinguish a treaty from domestic law. |
| Politics | Explain the decision and who can make it; show competing public interests. |
| Crime & Justice | Identify the procedural status; detention is not conviction; allegations are attributed. |
| Business & Economy | Read exclusions, identify who pays, and connect contracts to ordinary lives. |
| Science & Technology | Distinguish observation, replication and explanation; an unknown source is not a discovery of life. |
| Entertainment | Review the work and its labor; do not treat credits as proof of private relationships. |
| Sports | Report the canonical fixture score; calculate standings; keep an equipment review distinct from a changed result. |
| Culture & Lifestyle | Follow customs and choices through concrete scenes rather than gadget catalogs. |
| Investigations | State what documents establish, what they cannot establish and what evidence is still needed. |
| Opinion | Label judgment and author perspective; inherit the same factual constraints as reporting. |
| Local News | Return to the people affected after the first dramatic decision. |
| Oddities | Find modest surprise in everyday systems; avoid turning the entire publication into satire. |

## Images and identity

All twelve illustrations are original SVG source assets included in the repository. They depict environments, objects and a stylized sports scene. They are labeled editorial illustrations, never real photographs. People receive monograms until a reviewed canonical visual reference exists. A future image provider must retain each identity reference and require approved budget; it cannot silently substitute a new face.

## Corrections and retractions

Never silently rewrite published history. The correction action records the prior complete draft, reviewed replacement, actor, real timestamp and explanatory public note in append-only audit records. Publication dates remain unchanged. A retraction preserves the text at its original URL, displays a prominent notice, removes it from discovery and hides its community thread. Fixing prose does not mutate canon; a fact change requires a separately reviewed canonical event or future dedicated canon revision workflow.

## Editorial checklist

Before approving an event, inspect continuity warnings, jurisdiction, travel feasibility, causality and uncertainty. Before approving a draft, read the whole article against its events, check attribution, novelty, paragraph repetition, section voice, image reference and the current balance of desks and moods. Editing resets draft approval. Publication checks the event again under the database transaction. Publish only with the intentional final action.
