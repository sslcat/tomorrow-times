# World bible — Common Reach v1

A loose association of habitats whose lives are connected by slow ships, delayed messages and the difficult work of sharing enough.

This document is an editorial canon reference. Private facts and planned developments belong in the protected editorial desk, not public search. The versioned source is src/data/world.ts; the seed preserves it in database records. The launch edition is 3026; the live publication calendar advances independently.

## Physical and social rules

### ftl

No faster-than-light travel or communication exists.

### travel

Fusion ships follow scheduled corridors; distances, fuel and acceleration matter. No teleportation or faster-than-light communication.

### communication

Earth–Moon 0.03 minutes; Earth–Mars 4–24 minutes; inner system–Jupiter 35–55 minutes; Saturn 65–90 minutes. A relay cannot remove light delay.

### medicine

Typical life expectancy is 115–140 years where care is affordable; record 171. Cancer repair and organ growth are routine but unevenly available. No resurrection, mind backups or immortality.

### personhood

The Sentient Labor Accord of 2871 protects embodied and distributed machine persons. A certified continuity key identifies one legal person; copying a key does not create a new legal vote.

### currency

The clearing mark pays interhabitat debts. Local currencies include Martian hours and Ceres water shares; no currency makes life support free.

### jurisdiction

Local courts control criminal process. The Common Reach Council arbitrates cargo corridors and common reservoirs, not domestic elections. Warrants do not automatically cross borders.

### culture

Families keep departure suppers. Schools teach delay etiquette. Letters travel cheaply; bodies do not. Habitats observe Earth calendar dates alongside local shift calendars.

### economics

Water, repair labor, radiation shielding and transport insurance shape prices. Helix operates pumps; Meridian competes in freight and financing. Both depend on public corridors.

### politics

The Continuity caucus favors common reserves; the Hearth coalition favors local autonomy. Neither is uniformly virtuous. Earth restoration districts have no single planetary executive.

### sport

The Reach Six league plays tetherball in regulated low-gravity cages. A win earns three table points, a draw one; all scores are permanent fixture records.

### entertainment

Concerts are local performances or honestly labeled delayed relays. Liveness is measured at the source, not wherever a recording happens to arrive.

### education

Public apprenticeships are common. Habitat transfers often interrupt qualifications, tenancy and school enrollment.

## Inhabited places and jurisdictions

### Earth · Restoration Districts (earth)

A federation of restoration districts, rebuilt estuaries and stubborn old cities. Its delegates speak for districts, never for all humanity.

### Luna · Tranquility (luna)

The lunar civic assembly governs buried neighborhoods and open industrial yards. Rescue law is older than property law here.

### Ares Commons · Mars (ares)

The Commons elects a steward and a neighborhood assembly. The reservoir authority publishes reserve ledgers, but contractors retain maintenance logs.

### Valles Steps · Mars (valles)

A separate Martian jurisdiction with cooperative farms and a highly opinionated transit council. Two days from Ares by surface crawler.

### Ceres · The Reservoir Ring (ceres)

An elected ring board licenses reservoirs and barges. Dock courts hear contract disputes; family kitchens share communal warmth.

### Europa · Pelagos (europa)

The Pelagos research charter forbids drilling into the ocean pending contamination review. Scientists depend on repair crews as much as instruments.

### Ganymede · Juniper (ganymede)

Juniper holds the Jovian regional court and a network of protected agricultural chambers. Municipal citizenship includes a paid repair rotation.

### Titan · Lantern (titan)

Lantern governs insulated cavern settlements. Transport schedules, rather than Earth seasons, shape the social year.

### Venus · Morrow Aerostat (venus)

The aerostat compact governs floating habitats in the temperate cloud layer. Ballast rights are negotiated between towns every six months.

### Waystation Nine (waystation)

A rotating inner-belt interchange under Common Reach corridor law. Ships wait days for fuel; residents wait years for a proper school.

## Major institutions

- **Common Reach Council** (council; luna): A treaty chamber with broad responsibilities and narrow powers.
- **Ares Reservoir Authority** (water-board; ares): The public agency charged with publishing a reserve ledger every shift.
- **Helix Waterworks** (helix; ares): A pump contractor whose indispensable machines have made it politically difficult to question.
- **Meridian Transit** (meridian; ceres): A freight cooperative turned clearing-mark giant, competing to operate the Mars corridor.
- **Pelagos Listening Institute** (pelagos; europa): A research institute bound by the ocean noninterference charter.
- **Ceres Dock Service** (customs; ceres): Inspectors caught between scarce medicines, trade law and unsafe cargo.
- **Reach Six League** (reach-six; luna): Six clubs, sealed cages and a shared rulebook; the traveling league of tetherball.
- **Ceres Comets** (comets; ceres): A worker-owned tetherball club that pays its youth coaches before its directors.
- **Tranquility Saints** (saints; luna): A technically precise club with a famously impatient home crowd.
- **Red Ribbons** (ribbons; ares): Mars’s inventive cage team, known for slow starts and late comebacks.
- **Lantern Records** (lantern-records; titan): An artist cooperative negotiating the gap between local shows and interplanetary relays.
- **The Tomorrow Times** (tomorrow; luna): An independent fictional newsroom following public decisions into ordinary kitchens.
